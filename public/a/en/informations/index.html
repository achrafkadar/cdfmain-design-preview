New patients

New patients? You are welcome!

We are always happy to welcome new patients, young and old, into the big family of Clinique Dentaire Familiale Main.

To offer you personalized, top-quality care, it is important that we have a complete picture of your general and oral health. Information about your medical history, your medications and your dental history allows us to better assess your needs and plan the most appropriate treatments.

To make your first visit easier and optimize your time at the clinic, we invite you to fill out your medical-dental questionnaire online before your appointment. You can complete it at your convenience, from the comfort of your home, with all the necessary information at hand.

Children (from age 2)

Good dental habits start at a young age!

We welcome children from the age of 2 to guide them toward optimal oral health. An early first visit makes it possible to monitor the development of the teeth and jaws, prevent certain oral problems and establish good hygiene habits from childhood.

Our team is committed to offering a positive experience in a warm, reassuring environment adapted to little ones. We take the time to build a climate of trust so that your child becomes familiar with dental care and develops a healthy relationship with the dentist.

To make every visit even more enjoyable, a specially designed play area is available for children, allowing them to have fun and relax before their appointment.

Because a healthy smile starts with a positive first experience!

Dental insurance

Since information about your dental insurance plan is confidential, we do not have access to the details of your coverage. We therefore recommend that you contact your insurer before your appointment to find out which treatments are covered, the applicable limitations and the annual amount available. This will help you avoid any surprises regarding fees that may remain your responsibility.

To simplify the process, our team offers electronic submission of claims to your insurer. When we receive a real-time response during your visit, you only pay the portion not covered by your plan. If no response is obtained at the time of your visit, full payment of the fees will be requested and your insurer will then reimburse the amount to which you are entitled, according to the terms of your contract, either by cheque or direct deposit.
