Confidential medical-dental questionnaire

The information in this medical-dental questionnaire is required to provide dental care. Your dental record is protected by law and by professional secrecy. It is kept at the clinic, and only the dentist and authorized staff may consult it or make entries in it.

This questionnaire allows the dentist and staff to provide the best possible care and reduce the risk of medical complications. It is in the patient’s interest to answer it carefully and to inform the dentist of any change in their health.
